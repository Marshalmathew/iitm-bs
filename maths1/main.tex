Welcome to the \textbf{Mathematics for Data Science 1 (MDS1)} Comprehensive Textbook. This document is designed to serve as a complete, beginner-friendly resource to help you learn, practice, and master every single topic covered in the IIT Madras Foundational Level course.

\section*{Structure of this Guide}
The book is divided into \textbf{12 Chapters}, corresponding to the 12 weeks of the official course curriculum:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Chapters 1--4 (Quiz 1 Content):} Sets, Relations, Functions, Coordinate Geometry, Straight Lines, Quadratic Equations, and Polynomial Algebra.
    \item \textbf{Chapters 5--8 (Quiz 2 Content):} Functions, Logarithms, Exponential Equations, Sequences, Limits, Continuity, and the foundations of Differentiation.
    \item \textbf{Chapters 9--12 (End-Term Content):} Integration (area under a curve), and an extensive, algorithm-focused dive into Graph Theory and Shortest Path/MST Algorithms.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Key Design Elements}
Throughout this textbook, key ideas are categorized in visually distinct, colored cards:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textcolor{tealdef}{\textbf{Teal Cards}} contain definitions, terminology, and core concepts.
    \item \textcolor{primaryborder}{\textbf{Slate Blue Cards}} contain theorems, rules, and mathematical properties.
    \item \textcolor{purpleex}{\textbf{Purple Cards}} contain detailed step-by-step solved examples.
    \item \textcolor{coralformula}{\textbf{Orange Cards}} highlight crucial formulas you must commit to memory.
    \item \textcolor{yttext}{\textbf{Video Resource Cards}} provide direct clickable links to official IIT Madras NPTEL lecture videos and handpicked visual explanation videos.
\end{itemize}
