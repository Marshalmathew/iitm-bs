\chapter*{Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Introduction}

Welcome to \textbf{Statistics for Data Science 2 (SDS2)}. Building upon foundational descriptive statistics and univariate probability, this course delivers advanced statistical inference, joint probability distributions, parameter estimation (MLE, MME, Bayesian MAP), Central Limit Theorem applications, and hypothesis testing frameworks.

\section*{Structure of this Guide}
The course is divided into \textbf{12 Chapters}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Chapters 1--4 (Quiz 1 Content):} Joint & Marginal Discrete/Continuous Distributions, Independence, Functions of RVs, Covariance, and Correlation.
    \item \textbf{Chapters 5--8 (Quiz 2 Content):} Expectations of Functions, Mixed Distributions, Marginal/Conditional Densities, Sample Statistics, Law of Large Numbers (LLN), Moment Generating Functions (MGF), and Central Limit Theorem (CLT).
    \item \textbf{Chapters 9--12 (End-Term Content):} Method of Moments (MME), Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE), Bayesian Inference (Prior/Posterior/MAP), Confidence Intervals, Z/t Hypothesis Testing, and Likelihood Ratio Tests (LRT).
\end{itemize}
