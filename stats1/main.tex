\chapter*{Introduction}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Introduction}

Welcome to the \textbf{Statistics for Data Science 1 (SDS1)} Revision Textbook. This guide covers every topic from the 12-week IIT Madras foundational course, building from descriptive summaries of raw data all the way to classical probability distributions used in machine learning models.

\section*{Structure of this Guide}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Chapters 1--4 (Quiz 1 Content):} Data types, scales, descriptive statistics for categorical and numerical data, measures of central tendency, dispersion, and association between variables.
    \item \textbf{Chapters 5--8 (Quiz 2 Content):} Counting principles, permutations, combinations, sample spaces, probability axioms, conditional probability, independence, and Bayes' Theorem.
    \item \textbf{Chapters 9--12 (End-Term Content):} Random variables, PMF/CDF, expectation, variance, Binomial, Poisson, and continuous distributions (Uniform, Exponential).
\end{itemize}
